Professional Summary

Signal processing expert specializing in digital/analog precoding and optimization for next-generation wireless systems. Designed algorithms for multi-user MIMO-OFDM, satellite-terrestrial, and RIS-assisted architectures, and developed localization solutions using GNSS, Wi-Fi FTM, BLE, UWB, and AoA/PDoA. Hands-on in debugging code and systems to solve real-world problems.
