I am a wireless systems architect who designs signal processing algorithms for multi-antenna, multicarrier wireless systems, from problem formulation and algorithm design to production C/C++ and Python code.

At **Cisco Systems** (2022–present), I develop signal processing algorithms for enterprise wireless networks. My work there includes a GNSS receiver built on raw pseudorange measurements and Kalman filtering, angle-of-arrival estimation from phase-difference measurements across AP antennas, UWB time-difference-of-arrival (TDoA) positioning, and RF impairment analysis of transmit front-end modules. This work has produced seven patent families, including a granted U.S. patent, and I was recognized as a Cisco "Wireless Star."

My research covers the physical layer of **MIMO-OFDM** systems:

- **Hybrid digital/analog precoding and combining** for multi-user mmWave MIMO-OFDM, robust to phase-shifter phase errors
- **Joint spatial-spectral precoding** under clipping and out-of-band emission (spectral mask) constraints, at the block level and with per-symbol mask compliance
- **Hardware-impairment-aware design**, such as IQ-imbalance-aware precoding for RIS-assisted cell-free massive MIMO-OFDM
- **Large-scale optimization** for transceiver design: WMMSE, block coordinate descent, ADMM and Riemannian manifold methods, with convergence guarantees

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the **University of Minnesota** in 2022, advised by Prof. Zhi-Quan Luo.
